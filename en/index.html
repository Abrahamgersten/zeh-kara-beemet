What is "It Really Happened"?

"It Really Happened" is the name of the true tzaddik stories you can listen to here. Alongside them are more great recordings: the wonders of the world around us, what's happening in this week's Torah portion, a beautiful idea from it, interesting facts, and a fun picture game.

Next to every recording you'll find a few words about what it's about - so you know what you're about to hear, even before you press play.
